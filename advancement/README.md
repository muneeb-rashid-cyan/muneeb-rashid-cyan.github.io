## Why These Priorities

You are strong in *orchestration* (LangGraph, MCP, multi-agent) but the market is now
distinguishing between engineers who can **build agents** and those who can **make models
smarter** (fine-tuning, evals, reasoning). The second category commands significantly
higher salaries and is far less saturated.
